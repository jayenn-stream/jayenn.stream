Imagine a world of ten people on an island. In such a world, you have ten people of whom you can acquire human-serving products from. Sure, you can go to the nonhuman world and gain value-things. But humans uniquely can make certain products.     Now, you, using products you made, only have the lives of ten others to serve with your products, and thereby, ten sources of other persons human unique products to acquire. The best you can do is get a lot of things from 10 people.     But, in a world of more people, there are more to get from. A little gained from a lot becomes a lot.    That is why the richer get rich. Because there are more human lives they're getting from.     Therefore, more population of the world, more wealth in the scarce pot for each of us.     More life overall is more life for each.    Humans are inherently cobenefitting (assuming prolife dispositions).     This is why sex is pleasurable. More transformation of matter into human life, the more our lives, and our descendents lives, improve.
