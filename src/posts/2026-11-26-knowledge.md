Knowledge is justified true belief, it is said.     Is my sensory experience of any close object here now, a phone, a tv cabinet, etc. not knowledge of those things?     It is. So, that _knowledge_ originally spoken about must implicitly mean _propositional_ knowledge.     I vaguely recall SEP says this near the top. Yes it does.     On Gettier: "More particularly, the project of analysing knowledge is to state conditions that are individually necessary and jointly sufficient for propositional knowledge"
