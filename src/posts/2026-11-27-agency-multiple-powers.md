Agency is trying to acheive some value?     But trying may itself be determined. Question begging.     Agency, is having multiple powers, capabilities, multiple muscles to contract, multiple subjects to think about. The conditions are the same, practically.     That key qualifier is to hedge against a sort of absolute notion of sameness which no action relies upon. A cosmic sameness. There is none of that. So agency better not want that.
